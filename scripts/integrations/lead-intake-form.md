# SL Strength — Coaching Application & Assessment (recommended Google Form)

A single upgraded form that **captures → qualifies → assesses → books**. Build it
in Google Forms with the exact question titles below (they must match
`google-form-to-notion.gs`). `*` = required. Fields marked **→ CRM** map to a
dedicated Lead field; everything else lands in the "Assessment intake" Coach
Note on the lead.

> Form intro text (suggested): "Thanks for applying to SL Strength coaching.
> Answer honestly — it shapes your programming. Some assessments include a
> follow-up video chat, which we'll book at the end."

## Section 1 — About You
1. **Full name** * — short  → CRM **Name**
2. **Email** * — short answer, Response validation = Email  → CRM **Email**
3. **Phone** * — short  → CRM **Phone**
4. **Age** — short (number)
5. **Sex** — multiple choice: Male / Female / Prefer to self-describe
6. **How did you hear about us?** * — multiple choice: Instagram / Facebook / Referral / Website / Word of Mouth / Other  → CRM **Source**

## Section 2 — Goals & Fit
7. **What is your #1 goal?** * — short  → CRM **Goal**
8. **Why is now the right time? Any deadline or event?** — paragraph
9. **What have you tried before, and what got in the way?** — paragraph
10. **What are you most interested in?** * — checkboxes: Body Transformation / Strength / Nutrition / Hybrid  → CRM **Interest**
11. **Training experience** — multiple choice: Beginner (under 1 year) / Intermediate (1–3 years) / Advanced (3+ years)
12. **How many days per week can you train?** — multiple choice: 2 / 3 / 4 / 5+
13. **What equipment do you have access to?** — multiple choice: Full gym / Home basics (dumbbells/bands) / Bodyweight only
14. **What monthly investment range works for you?** — multiple choice: Under $100 / $100–$250 / $250–$500 / $500+  → CRM **Est. Value**

## Section 3 — Health & Readiness
15. **Any injuries, pain, or surgeries we should know about?** * — paragraph  → CRM **Problem**
16. **Any medical conditions or medications?** — paragraph
17. **Have you been cleared by a physician to exercise?** * — multiple choice: Yes / No / Unsure
18. **Average sleep per night (hours)** — short (number)
19. **Current stress level (1 low – 5 high)** — multiple choice: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5
20. **Nutrition snapshot (meals/day, protein, water, alcohol, restrictions)** — paragraph

## Section 4 — Movement Screen
21. **Can you stand on one foot for 10+ seconds? (try both feet)** * — multiple choice: Yes – both / One leg only / No
22. **Can you hold a plank for 2 minutes?** * — multiple choice: Yes / No
23. **Do you exercise at least 30 minutes most days?** — multiple choice: Yes / Sometimes / No
24. **Describe your current exercise routine** — paragraph
25. **Did you feel any pain during these movements? If so, where?** — paragraph
26. *(optional)* **Upload a short movement video** — File upload (requires respondent Google sign-in)

## Section 5 — Book Your Consult
> Section description (suggested): "Last step — book your free video consult:
> <paste your Calendly / Google Appointments link here>."
27. **Preferred consult date** — Date  → CRM **Consult Date**
28. **Preferred time of day** — multiple choice: Morning / Afternoon / Evening
29. **Is it okay to text and email you?** * — multiple choice: Yes / No

## Notes

  now surfaces via the movement screen + pain question.
- Split the old combined "phone or email" field into separate **Email** (validated)
  and **Phone** so leads are reliably contactable and map cleanly.
- Turn on **Settings → Responses → Collect email addresses = Do not collect**
  (you're asking Email explicitly) to avoid a duplicate email question, OR set it
  to "Responder input" and delete question #2 — either works; just keep one email.
